# Phase 1 — Canonical Marketing Domain Contracts

This phase adds an additive, universal marketing domain contract layer.

It does not remove or replace the existing 71 specialist agents or 13 workstreams.

## Introduced

- Canonical marketing business objects:
  CompanyContext, MarketingGoal, ICPProfile, PersonaProfile, Account, Contact, Signal, Campaign, Interaction, Lead, Opportunity, Customer, Outcome, MarketingMemoryRecord, Experiment, Recommendation.
- The 14 project-2 domain leaders.
- The 28 project-2 capabilities.
- Contract tests that freeze the imported project-2 catalog.

## Design rule

The 14 domain leaders are not duplicates of the 71 specialist agents. They are domain-level owners above the specialist workforce.

Next phases will connect these contracts to the canonical registry, Marketing Commander, memory/RAG, acquisition graph, and durable workflow runtime.
