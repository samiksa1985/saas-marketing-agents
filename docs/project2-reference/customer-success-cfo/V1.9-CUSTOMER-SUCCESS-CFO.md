# V1.9 — Customer Success + CFO Intelligence + Client Profitability

## Objective
Connect customer health, delivery outcomes, revenue, costs, margin, churn, renewal and expansion.

## Customer Success
- Customer health scoring
- onboarding status
- delivery/SLA health
- outcome tracking
- renewal risk
- expansion opportunities
- customer success actions
- QBR/MoM review foundations

## CFO Intelligence
- revenue
- recurring revenue
- COGS
- AI cost
- tool cost
- delivery cost
- gross profit
- gross margin
- operating expenses
- contribution margin
- CAC
- LTV
- break-even
- cash-flow foundation
- budget vs actual foundation
- scenario analysis

## Client profitability
Profitability is calculated per customer and package:
Revenue - AI Cost - Tool Cost - Delivery Cost = Gross Contribution.

Metrics are labeled ACTUAL / ESTIMATED / MODELED.

## Guardrails
- Never invent financial values.
- Missing source data must be marked missing or estimated.
- Financial recommendations require evidence.
- Customer risk scores are recommendations, not facts.
