# Customer Success Workflows

## At-risk customer
Health score → risk classification → recovery task → owner → deadline → review.

## Renewal
Days-to-renewal → health check → outcome review → renewal opportunity → proposal → approval.

## Upsell
Usage/results → unmet need → eligible add-on → expansion opportunity → Sales handoff.

## Monthly review
Customer outcomes → campaign performance → commercial impact → next actions → QBR summary.
