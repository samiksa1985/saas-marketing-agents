# Golden Workflow — New Client to First Marketing Campaign

## Objective
Move a newly onboarded client from raw business information to a reviewed, measurable first campaign without bypassing tenant isolation or approval gates.

## Flow
1. Client registration
2. Conversational onboarding
3. Business Intelligence builds Company Intelligence
4. Market Research validates market/competitor assumptions
5. Marketing Strategist creates 30/60/90-day strategy
6. Campaign Agent creates campaign plan
7. Creative Agent creates concepts and test variants
8. Content Agent creates channel assets
9. SEO Agent creates landing/search recommendations when applicable
10. AI QA validates factual, brand, strategic, and policy compliance
11. Internal approval
12. Client approval
13. Publish/launch through approved integration
14. Analytics Agent establishes baseline and monitors performance
15. Analytics Agent recommends optimization
16. Customer Success Agent updates client health
17. CFO Intelligence tracks delivery economics
18. AI Business Mentor summarizes lessons for the internal operator

## Approval Gates
- No external publication before client approval.
- No financial changes before authorized approval.
- No paid-media spend changes without configured permission.
- No external communication without an approved channel and permission.
