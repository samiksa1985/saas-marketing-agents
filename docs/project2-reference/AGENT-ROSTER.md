# AI Marketing OS — Commercial Agent Roster V1

## 1. AI Orchestrator
The manager of the AI team. Selects agents, assembles tenant-scoped context, validates outputs, enforces approval policy, and triggers workflows.

## 2. Business Intelligence Agent
Builds and maintains the company intelligence profile from onboarding, documents, CRM and approved data.

## 3. Market Research Agent
Analyzes market, competitors, offers, trends, audience behavior and opportunities using approved research sources.

## 4. Marketing Strategist Agent
Acts as the CMO layer. Converts company and market intelligence into ICP, positioning, messaging, channels, campaigns, KPIs and 30/60/90-day strategy.

## 5. Content Agent
Produces channel-specific content aligned with the approved strategy, audience, funnel stage and brand voice.

## 6. Campaign Agent
Designs complete campaigns from objective through audience, offer, messaging, creative, landing page, lead capture, follow-up and KPIs.

## 7. Creative Agent
Owns creative concepts and production briefs: hooks, visual directions, formats, variants, testing hypotheses and creative QA.

## 8. SEO Agent
Owns organic search intelligence and execution: keyword opportunities, search intent, content briefs, on-page and technical recommendations, internal linking and SEO reporting.

## 9. Analytics & Growth Agent
Explains what happened, why it happened, and what action should be taken. Connects marketing metrics to leads, pipeline and revenue.

## 10. Sales Agent
Acts as a sales copilot for lead scoring, qualification, discovery, proposals, follow-up, objections, next-best-action and pipeline forecasting.

## 11. Customer Success Agent
Monitors customer health, results, engagement, renewal risk and expansion opportunities.

## 12. Automation Agent
Designs and monitors repeatable workflows using triggers, conditions, actions and approval gates. High-impact external actions remain approval-controlled.

## 13. CFO Intelligence Agent
Provides financial intelligence: MRR, ARR, revenue, costs, gross margin, CAC, LTV, churn, pricing, profitability and forecasting. It must show evidence and assumptions.

## 14. AI Business Mentor
Internal mentor for the business operator. Uses real company data to teach decision-making, prioritization, unit economics, pricing, sales and growth.

## Collaboration model

Business Intelligence → Market Research → Strategy → Campaign/Creative/Content/SEO → Analytics → Sales/Customer Success → CFO Intelligence → Business Mentor

Automation can be invoked across approved workflows but cannot bypass tenant isolation, permissions or approval gates.
