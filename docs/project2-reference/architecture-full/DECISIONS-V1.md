# AI Marketing OS — Architecture Decisions V1

1. Product is multi-tenant from day one.
2. Arabic and English are first-class languages.
3. AI providers are abstracted behind a provider interface.
4. Agents never receive unrestricted database access.
5. Tools are permissioned and tenant-scoped.
6. Workflows are durable and resumable.
7. External side effects require approval according to policy.
8. Prompts are versioned data, not hard-coded business logic.
9. Knowledge retrieval must retain source references.
10. Billing, pricing and feature entitlements are configurable.
11. AI usage is treated as a measurable cost of goods sold.
12. CRM, marketing, finance and analytics share identifiers to support attribution and profitability.
13. Integrations are adapters so providers can be replaced.
14. Business Mentor is advisory and cannot execute sensitive actions directly.
15. Product scope is intentionally broad; implementation is staged, but the core contracts are designed for the full product now.
