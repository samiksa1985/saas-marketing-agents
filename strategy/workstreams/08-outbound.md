# Workstream 08: Trigger-Led Outbound

> **Status:** Execution brief; sequence, offer, language, and qualification hypotheses remain subject to validation.
>
> **Evidence boundary:** No company data, customer proof, pricing, performance benchmark, legal conclusion, local-access claim, or delivery capability is supplied here. Keep unknowns as `[NEEDS INPUT]` or `Unknown` until sourced and approved.

## Objective

Turn the approved ABM account set into a small, signal-led outbound motion that earns relevant conversations and produces accepted seller handoffs. The unit of work is an account with a dated trigger, a reachable role, a clear suppression status, an evidence-backed problem hypothesis, and a next commercial milestone.

Outbound does not optimize for contact volume or activity volume. It tests which trigger, account segment, buyer role, offer, language, and channel combination creates qualified commercial progression. It stops or parks accounts that lack evidence, permission, owner capacity, or a credible next step.

### Required contact record

Research role before person. Capture only what is necessary and permitted:

- Name, title, company, professional profile or source, source date, and confidence.
- Role in the buying committee: economic buyer, champion/commercial owner, operator, influencer, blocker, or unknown.
- Reachable channel and its permission/eligibility status; never assume that a public profile grants permission for every channel.
- Relevant buyer-language or company-specific context, with source and date.
- Last touch, next allowed action, cooling-off status, and contact-level suppression result.
- Preferred or observed language context only when sourced; do not infer language from nationality, name, or location.

## Trigger and Research Protocol

### Trigger standard

A trigger is actionable only when it is dated, sourced, relevant to the account's commercial situation, and connected to a plausible buyer role. Examples to investigate, not to assume:

- A new offer, branch, market, partnership, event, hiring change, or commercial initiative.
- A stated growth, utilization, pipeline, or market-entry objective.
- A first-party reply, meeting request, detailed question, or repeat engagement where the system records it reliably.
- A warm introduction or referral with consent and context.

An external signal alone is not buying intent. Pair fit with a reachable role and, before a bespoke Tier 1 play, a second independent signal family. Undated signals have no action value. Active opportunities, live sales conversations, opt-outs, legal restrictions, known customers where applicable, and recent touches are suppression conditions, not triggers.

### Research pass

Before drafting a touch, the researcher records:

1. What the account sells and the evidence that it serves B2B buyers.
2. The dated trigger, source URL or note, access date, evidence label, and freshness decision.
3. One problem hypothesis tied to the trigger; separate observation from interpretation.
4. Two or more possible roles, researched role-first and labeled known, hypothesized, or missing.
5. The alternative or status quo likely relevant to the account: internal ownership, fragmented providers, generalist agency, referrals/partners, software, local provider, or another sourced route.
6. One approved or directional message angle from positioning/messaging, and the proof or capability needed before using it.
7. One low-friction offer and the intended next milestone.
8. Suppression, channel eligibility, language-review needs, and seller availability.

Stop and park when two research passes produce no dated trigger, reachable relevant role, or credible reason for a timely conversation. Record the missing evidence and reconsideration condition.

## Offer Architecture

Outbound leads with a useful, bounded conversation or diagnostic. It does not promise leads, revenue, performance, local access, or a free bespoke deliverable unless those items are approved and actually available.

| Offer hypothesis | Best use | Required before activation |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition Leakage Diagnostic | Account has a visible handoff, follow-up, qualification, or pipeline-visibility trigger. | `[NEEDS INPUT: scope, inputs, owner, output, time commitment, and approval]` |
| Revenue Workflow Review | Account has fragmented marketing/sales ownership or a visible process change. | `[NEEDS INPUT: review method, deliverable, client responsibilities, and proof boundary]` |
| Pipeline Reset Pilot | Account has one defined offer, buyer, commercial owner, and a time-bound motion worth testing. | `[NEEDS INPUT: actual package, duration, price, delivery capacity, exit criteria, and contract]` |
| AI Acquisition Readiness Review | Account is explicitly evaluating AI-supported acquisition or workflow controls. | `[NEEDS INPUT: AI workflow, data handling, human-review controls, and approved claims]` |

The offer brief for every sequence must state: problem addressed, intended role, inputs requested, output, human owner, data boundary, approval status, next step, and what it does not promise. If the actual offer differs, replace the hypothesis rather than silently expanding scope.

## Sequence

Use a maximum of five active touches over roughly two to three weeks. Timing is an operating default, not a performance benchmark. The seller may shorten, pause, or stop the sequence when a person responds, opts out, becomes an active opportunity, or asks for a different path.

| Order | Default timing | Touch | Purpose and gate |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1 | Trigger-led email or approved warm introduction | Name the sourced trigger, one problem hypothesis, and one relevant offer. Keep it concise and specific. Do not claim the account has a problem as fact. |
| 2 | Day 3 | Useful follow-up by the same approved channel | Add one workflow observation, alternative-aware insight, or question. Offer two concrete discovery options only if the seller can honor them. |
| 3 | Day 7 | Approved LinkedIn or phone touch, where permitted | Use the same account context, not a generic “following up” message. Phone voicemail, if allowed, is brief and identifies the sender and reason. |
| 4 | Day 10 | Role or committee expansion | Contact a second relevant stakeholder only when the account brief supports the role and the channel is cleared. Do not use this to pressure the first contact or bypass a stated preference. |
| 5 | Day 14-21 | Close-the-loop message | Offer a clear “not now,” referral, or permission-based nurture path. Stop active outreach after this touch unless the contact re-engages or gives permission for a different cadence. |

### Tier service levels

- **Tier 1:** One-to-one account brief, corroborated trigger, senior seller involvement, and a tailored point of view. No bespoke asset before a conversation confirms the problem.
- **Tier 2:** Shared cluster message plus one sourced account line, with a named seller and backup route where permitted.
- **Tier 3:** Light personalization based on sourced segment, role, and trigger; shared educational or event offer. No bespoke promise.

A positive reply, meeting request, or detailed question exits automation and creates a human seller task. An active opportunity exits outbound and is owned by the deal team. A no-response contact is not a qualified lead and does not receive an expanded sequence merely because a trigger remains interesting.

## Message and Offer Testing

### Arabic/English test

Test Arabic-first, English-first, and bilingual variants only where the account context, role, channel, and reviewer support the test. The test compares the same offer, trigger class, audience definition, and sequence step; language is the variable, not a hidden change to proof or CTA.

For each variant, record:

- Account segment, tier, role, trigger family, sequence version, language, reviewer, and launch date.
- Exact approved copy version and the message hypothesis.
- Delivery status, replies, positive replies, meetings requested, meetings held, qualified conversations, opportunities, opt-outs, complaints, and seller feedback where available.
- Any interpretation or translation issue, with the correction owner.

Use qualified replies, meetings held, accepted handoffs, and opportunities as the decision signals. Opens and send-time patterns are directional deliverability observations only; do not use them as the conversion base or to crown a language winner. Do not declare a winner when the segment cannot produce enough human outcomes to discriminate; record the test as inconclusive.

Arabic customer-facing copy requires `[NEEDS INPUT: qualified Arabic reviewer and approval path]`. Machine translation alone is not approval. Preserve a role/segment-specific language decision rather than treating Arabic or English as a universal market preference.

### Message controls

Every message must map to the approved positioning and message architecture. Before launch, check:

- The trigger and account observation are sourced and not overstated.
- The message names a buyer problem, alternative, or useful question rather than leading with unsupported AI claims.
- Any proof, customer reference, metric, pricing, local-access, capability, or outcome statement has an approved source and scope.
- The CTA has one clear response path and matches seller capacity.
- The copy does not expose private research, sensitive data, or a guessed personal detail.
- The language variant has passed the required human review.

## Channel and Consent Guardrails

This workstream does not decide what is lawful. Before every touch and in every channel:

1. Query the single cross-channel suppression record owned by `ops-legal-compliance`.
2. Confirm the account/contact is not a customer, active opportunity, live sales conversation, legal hold, recent-touch conflict, or other declared no-touch condition.
3. Confirm the channel, geography, data source, purpose, and consent/permission posture are cleared for the specific contact and message.
4. Record source, collection/access date, channel status, reviewer, next action, and opt-out instructions where required.
5. Apply an opt-out immediately across the sequence and any matched audiences or other outbound channels; do not maintain a competing local list.
6. Escalate uncertainty about email, LinkedIn, phone, WhatsApp, cross-border data, privacy, recording, or platform rules to `ops-legal-compliance` before sending.

Do not treat account importance, public contact information, a referral, or a platform connection as automatic permission for every channel. Do not send through WhatsApp, automate LinkedIn activity, or use a purchased list unless the channel and data posture are specifically approved. `email-deliverability-specialist` advises on sending health; it does not clear consent.

## Qualification and Seller Handoff

### Qualification exit criteria

A positive reply is an engagement signal, not a qualified opportunity. The seller records whether the account has:

- ICP fit and a defined B2B offer.
- A specific acquisition, qualification, follow-up, or booking problem.
- A current trigger or timing reason to evaluate it.
- A commercial owner and plausible economic buyer.
- Enough demand, sales capacity, delivery capacity, or access to test a motion.
- Willingness to provide the baseline process or evidence needed for the next step.
- Known data, privacy, procurement, integration, and delivery constraints, or explicit owners for resolving them.
- An agreed next step, owner, and date.

Nurture or disqualify when authority, timing, evidence access, delivery capacity, problem severity, or a credible next step is absent. Never force a weak response into pipeline.

### Handoff contract

| Event | Outbound action | Seller acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Positive reply or meeting request | Stop automated touches, create a task, send the full account/contact evidence pack, and notify the paired seller. | Seller responds or accepts the task within one business day, records disposition, and owns the next step. |
| Meeting booked | Provide trigger source, message version, language, offer, contact role, committee gaps, suppression status, and discovery hypothesis. | Seller confirms agenda, attendees, qualification questions, and next milestone. |
| Meeting held | Capture outcome, qualification state, objection, alternative, and next date. | Accepted handoff becomes a CRM stage only when the qualification exit criteria are met. |
| Active opportunity | Remove account/contact from outbound automation and transfer to `sales-deal-strategist`. | Deal owner confirms acceptance and records source and primary credit. |
| Not now / timing gap | Record reason, permission/status, owner, nurture path, and next eligible date. | Seller or nurture owner accepts the future task; no active-sequence restart without a valid trigger or permission. |
| Opt-out, complaint, or legal concern | Suppress immediately and route the record/question to `ops-legal-compliance`. | Suppression is visible across channels; no re-entry without an approved basis. |

Route message, proof, role, or objection gaps to `pmm-messaging-architect` and positioning. Route field, routing, stage, attribution, and reporting gaps to `analytics-marketing-ops-architect`. Outbound retains the sequence decision; Sales retains the commercial decision.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every active account has a sourced, dated trigger, fit rationale, relevant role hypothesis, owner, next milestone, confidence, and current suppression status.
- No contact enters a sequence without channel-specific eligibility and a cross-channel suppression check recorded before the touch.
- The active list fits the documented seller and research capacity; parked, blocked, and retired accounts are visible with reasons.
- Each sequence names its trigger, segment/tier, role, offer, channel, language, ordered touches, stop conditions, cooling-off rule, and owner.
- Every offer is bounded by approved scope, inputs, outputs, data handling, and non-promises; no invented pricing, proof, customer data, performance, or legal conclusion appears.
- Arabic/English variants use the same offer and test conditions, receive qualified human review, and are judged on human commercial outcomes rather than opens.
- Positive replies and meeting requests exit automation; the seller receives the evidence pack and accepts or dispositions the handoff within the agreed SLA.
- Qualification requires fit, problem, authority, timing, evidence access, constraints, and a dated next step; a reply alone is not pipeline.
- Active opportunities, opt-outs, legal restrictions, customers where applicable, and recent-touch conflicts have documented suppression or deal-owner outcomes.
- The scoreboard distinguishes touches, human responses, accepted handoffs, qualified conversations, opportunities, and primary-source pipeline credit; it does not use open rate as a conversion denominator.
- Feedback from sellers, Messaging, Positioning, Competitive Intelligence, Analytics, Deliverability, and Legal is routed with an owner and due date.
- A review decision records continue, revise, pause, park, or stop, the evidence behind it, unresolved uncertainty, and the next test.
