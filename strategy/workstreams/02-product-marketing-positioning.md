# Workstream 02: Product Marketing / Positioning

> **Status:** Execution artifact for validation. Not approved public positioning.
>
> **Evidence boundary:** This workstream contains hypotheses and required validation work. It includes no fabricated customer evidence, proof, pricing, performance, market-size, local-access, compliance, or capability claims.

## Objective

Select and document a defensible positioning frame for the Saudi B2B customer acquisition service, starting with the recommended H1 hypothesis, and produce the evidence-backed inputs needed for messaging, sales, channel, and offer decisions.

The workstream must determine whether the market understands and values an accountable outsourced acquisition department, what alternative it replaces for a target buyer, which claims the actual delivery model can sustain, and what proof or qualification is required before public use. Until the decision gate is passed, H1 remains a working hypothesis rather than a market claim.

### Comparative message test

Test the same offer context with:

- **H1:** Outsourced B2B acquisition department.
- **H2:** AI-native pipeline operating partner.
- **H3:** Saudi market-entry and demand validation partner, only for accounts with a relevant launch or expansion trigger.

Record comprehension, problem recognition, differentiation, trust/risk, commercial relevance, and next-step intent. Do not choose a winner from a small click-rate difference or raw response volume alone.

7. **Sales enablement input:** discovery prompts, qualification implications, objection responses, and handoff language.
8. **Validation backlog:** experiments, owner, sample, decision threshold, and next review date.

## Acceptance Criteria

The workstream is accepted when:

- The selected position is explicitly marked **validated**, **directional**, or **held**, with the evidence supporting that status.
- H1, H2, and relevant H3 tests include buyer role, segment, sample/source, stimulus version, date, and observed result.
- The primary segment, buyer, job, alternative, anti-ICP, and loss conditions are documented from evidence or clearly marked `[NEEDS INPUT]`.
- Each material differentiator is tied to an actual capability, operating owner, and approved proof source; unsupported claims are removed or blocked.
- The positioning statement and message variants do not contain fabricated customer data, pricing, performance, market size, local access, regulatory authority, or customer proof.
- AI language states the human accountability and control boundary, with data and security questions routed to Legal.
- Saudi-specific language is supported by segment evidence and qualified review, or remains a research question.
- Sales can use the package in a discovery conversation, and the handoff contract includes owner, input, acceptance criteria, escalation trigger, and feedback loop.
- Messaging, ABM/Segmentation, Sales, Ops/Measurement, Legal, and Customer Proof recipients have accepted their handoffs.
- The sponsor has recorded a promote, revise, or hold decision and a next review date.
