## 1. Objective and role

Establish a founder-led LinkedIn operating loop that turns sourced account and buyer insight into credible public points of view, relevant account conversations, and measurable progression from `FIND` through `QUALIFY`, without making the founder perform as a content department or treating social activity as pipeline by default.

The first paid engagement is a learning motion, not a publishing quota. The channel must answer:

1. Which approved Saudi B2B account segments and buyer roles recognize the problem being discussed?
2. Which language and message variants produce substantive, permission-consistent conversations?
3. Which conversations progress to an accepted sales or discovery next step?
4. Can the founder sustain the operating cadence and response SLA without compromising delivery or judgment?

### LinkedIn/social owns

- Founder profile and company-page organic execution for the approved motion.
- A small, evidence-backed content and conversation queue tied to named account tiers and buyer roles.
- Drafting, editing, approval routing, publishing coordination, comment/reply handling, and escalation.
- Human engagement with relevant public conversations where the founder has a genuine contribution.
- Arabic-first, English-first, and bilingual message tests with language metadata and native review.
- Channel event capture and a weekly evidence pack for account progression and measurement.

### LinkedIn/social does not own

- Selecting the target-account list, tier, fit score, trigger, or suppression decision; `01-icp-abm` owns those.
- Validating positioning, material claims, proof, or comparative language; `02-product-marketing-positioning` and Legal own those gates.
- Manufacturing engagement through pods, reciprocal comments, fake accounts, bulk automation, scraped personal data, or generic tagging.
- Replacing discovery, qualification, proposal work, sales follow-up, or customer support.
- Treating followers, impressions, reactions, views, or comments as qualified demand.
- Running LinkedIn paid media, Thought Leader Ads, dark posts, or audience delivery; paid execution belongs to the paid-media owners.

## 4. First-engagement operating model

### Account and conversation scope

Use the active list and tiers from `01-icp-abm`. Do not build a competing target list from LinkedIn search. For each active account, record:

- Account ID, tier, sourced fit reason, source/date, trigger, and confidence.
- Relevant founder, economic buyer, champion, operator, blocker, or influencer role; label each `known`, `hypothesized`, or `missing`.
- Public profile URL and account evidence only where lawfully visible and permitted; no sensitive personal inference.
- Conversation hypothesis tied to a real trigger or documented problem, not a generic persona assumption.
- Suppression state, recent-touch check, paired owner, next milestone, and expiry/review date.

The founder may engage a public post because it is relevant and useful even when the account is not yet a named target, but the account cannot enter the active commercial motion until ABM applies the same evidence, ownership, and suppression rules.

### Content and conversation loop

1. **Research:** ABM or the research owner supplies a sourced account/cluster signal, buyer-language observation, customer question, or approved learning.
2. **Brief:** Social owner states audience role, stage intent, single point of view, evidence boundary, language variant, desired response, and no-go claims.
3. **Draft:** Founder voice is preserved. The draft gives a useful observation, framework, teardown, experiment result, or question before any offer reference.
4. **Review:** Positioning/claims, Arabic/bilingual quality, legal/privacy, and factual QA are applied according to risk.
5. **Publish or reply:** Founder publishes or replies from the approved profile. The first comment, if used, is substantive context, not bait.
6. **Respond:** Comments are triaged by intent and routed within the agreed SLA. The founder handles perspective and relationship; sales handles commercial discovery.
7. **Learn:** Record account progression, language, response type, objections, and next action. Feed verified learning to positioning, ABM, content, sales, and measurement.

## 5. Content formats and selection rules

Choose a format because it makes the point clearer or more useful, not because a platform format is assumed to receive more reach.

| Format | Use in the first engagement | Required input / control |
|---|---|---|
| Founder text post | One sourced operating observation, decision framework, failure mode, or experiment note | One claim or question; source/evidence tag; no unsupported result claim |
| Document/carousel | A compact checklist, workflow map, comparison dimensions, or diagnostic sequence | Approved content/design input; every page has a source or is labeled hypothesis |
| Native video | Founder explanation of one workflow or buyer question where voice/clarity matters | Recording consent if anyone else appears; transcript/caption QA; approved claims |
| Comment/reply | Direct answer to a relevant buyer or practitioner discussion | Genuine expertise; disclosure if affiliation is material; no hijack or pitch-first reply |
| Founder reshare with commentary | Add a distinct Saudi B2B operating implication to a source or partner post | Source attribution; permission/rights check; commentary must stand on its own |
| Company-page post | Durable approved asset, proof-cleared announcement, or practical resource | Page admin, link/UTM QA, claims approval, response owner |
| Direct message | Continue a conversation after a relevant public interaction or explicit permission | Context, consent/permission state, account owner, no bulk sequence, clear opt-out path |

The initial queue should contain no more than the capacity approved by the founder. `[NEEDS INPUT: weekly founder hours, maximum posts/replies, and protected response windows]`. A smaller queue with complete review and follow-up is preferable to an unmaintainable calendar.

### Working content territories

Use the master GTM narrative and Workstreams 02–03 to select topics:

- Acquisition leakage and handoff quality.
- Revenue workflow, qualification, response, and evidence discipline.
- Saudi execution questions where the account-specific evidence supports them.
- Applied AI workflows with explicit human review and data boundaries.
- Experiments, limitations, alternatives, and failure modes.

Do not call a topic a Saudi buyer truth, category trend, or performance pattern without sourced evidence. Record it as a hypothesis and define the discriminating check.

## 6. Account engagement and response routing

### Engagement rules

- Read the full source conversation and community context before replying.
- Add a concrete observation, criterion, example, or useful question; do not use empty praise or engagement bait.
- Engage from the founder's real experience and disclose company affiliation when the relationship or claim would not otherwise be obvious.
- Do not mass-connect, scrape, auto-comment, copy the same reply across accounts, or use connection acceptance as consent for a sales sequence.
- Do not tag a person or company unless the reference is relevant and expected in context.
- Stop or cool off after a clear decline, opt-out, legal restriction, active opportunity handoff, or stated no-contact request.
- Replies to comments on an owned post are due within `[NEEDS INPUT: approved response SLA]`; the working assumption is one business day, subject to founder availability.

### Response routing table

| Signal | Immediate action | Owner / destination |
|---|---|---|
| General reaction or thanks | Acknowledge only if useful; no commercial escalation | Founder / social owner |
| Substantive question or disagreement | Founder answers from approved evidence; log the learning | Founder; route claim gap to Positioning/Legal |
| Request for resource | Share approved asset with tracked path and context; record account/person ID if known | Social owner + Marketing Ops |
| Problem disclosure without buying intent | Give a useful answer; ask permission before continuing privately | Founder; Insights if research permission exists |
| Explicit request to discuss, demo, audit, or scope | Same-business-day acknowledgement; create or update account activity and route | Paired seller / `sales-outbound-strategist` |
| Existing opportunity or customer | Stop channel selling; hand to deal/customer owner and suppress duplicate outreach | `sales-deal-strategist` or Customer Marketing `[NEEDS INPUT]` |
| Opt-out, privacy, legal, or data request | Do not debate publicly; preserve minimum routing context and escalate | `ops-legal-compliance` + suppression owner |
| Complaint, safety issue, or reputational escalation | Screenshot/context preservation; no improvised defense | QA + Legal + designated comms owner `[NEEDS INPUT]` |
| Unknown or suspicious account | Do not infer identity or intent; park pending verification | ABM + QA |

A positive social signal is not a QUALIFY exit. It becomes QUALIFY only when the GTM exit criteria are documented in the CRM and a named seller accepts the handoff.

## 7. Language testing

Test language as a decision variable, not as a universal assumption. Use matched or comparable topic briefs across Arabic-first, English-first, and bilingual variants, while recording role, sector, account tier, public profile language, device/context where available, and approval reviewer.

### Test design

- Pre-register the hypothesis, variant, audience/account slice, format, CTA, observation window, and primary progression event.
- Hold the commercial point of view and offer context as constant as practical; change language and only one additional variable at a time.
- Use qualified native Saudi Arabic review for customer-facing Arabic. Machine translation may assist drafting but is not final review.
- Track meaningful replies, accepted conversations, meetings held, qualification quality, and objections. Reach or reactions may diagnose distribution but cannot decide the winner.
- Preserve an `Unknown` language bucket. Do not infer preference from name, nationality, location, or profile photo.
- Do not declare a winner from a small or mixed sample. `[NEEDS INPUT: minimum comparable account/person cohort, maturity window, and decision owner]`.
- Feed language-specific buyer wording and objections back to Positioning, Content, Sales, and Insights.

## 8. Approval and quality control

### Approval levels

| Risk level | Examples | Required approval |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Founder opinion, sourced operational question, generic framework with no product/proof claim | Founder + social owner + factual QA |
| Medium | Offer reference, CTA, named alternative, customer/problem example, Arabic/bilingual asset | Founder + Positioning/Messaging + native reviewer + QA |
| High | Customer proof, quantified outcome, legal/privacy/data statement, regulated-sector claim, competitor comparison, crisis response | Founder + Positioning + Legal/Privacy + QA; customer/proof owner where relevant |

No item moves to scheduled or published status with unresolved material claims, missing source, missing language review, unclear response owner, broken tracking, or an active suppression conflict. Page and profile credentials remain with the approved account administrator; the workstream does not request or store passwords in the document.

QA records version, reviewer, date, evidence links, UTM/campaign ID, disclosure text where needed, and expiry/review date. Approved does not mean permanently valid: stale proof, changed offer, changed policy, or changed account context reopens review.

## 9. Measurement contract

### Primary unit and events

Use the immutable account ID from the measurement system. Store person activity as account-linked activity where possible. Required event fields:

- Account ID, person ID where available, profile/page URL, owner, tier, and buying role.
- Post/reply ID, format, topic, language variant, version, publication/reply timestamp in UTC, and reporting time zone Asia/Riyadh.
- Source class `organic-social`, platform, campaign/content ID, UTM fields for owned links, and consent/permission state where applicable.
- Event type: view/click if available, meaningful comment, direct reply, connection/request state, resource request, meeting request, meeting held, qualification, nurture, proposal, conversion, opt-out, or suppression.
- Evidence label, source link, confidence, next action, routing timestamp, acceptance timestamp, and CRM stage timestamp.

### Report separately

- **Delivery diagnostics:** impressions, reach, views, reactions, comments, follower/connection movement, click-through, first-hour activity, and post format. These describe distribution; they do not establish buyer quality.
- **Leading commercial signals:** target-account engagement, substantive replies, accepted conversations, resource requests, meeting requests, meetings held, response SLA, and account-stage movement.
- **Lagging commercial outcomes:** QUALIFY, PROPOSE, CONVERT, qualified pipeline, booked revenue, gross margin, and payback using the master measurement definitions.
- **Attribution views:** sourced first eligible social touch, influenced social touches, and the declared management-allocation view. Never sum influenced pipeline across channels.

For each metric show numerator, denominator, exclusions, cohort age, source system, target version, actual, variance, unknown count, and next action. Do not import LinkedIn benchmarks or claim that an early engagement pattern predicts reach until this account's own comparable posts test it. Link placement and employee notifications are hypotheses to test on the account, not standing rules.

### Guardrails and decision gates

- Pause optimization conclusions when required fields/events are incomplete, account linking is unreliable, suppression is unresolved, or CRM stage timestamps cannot be reconstructed.
- Investigate tracking, follow-up, account mix, and language before calling a content or channel failure.
- Do not scale a format, language, or topic from reactions alone; require a pre-registered progression signal and a mature enough cohort.
- Record a scale, iterate, hold, or stop decision with evidence and a next test at the Day 30, 60, and 90 GTM gates.

## 12. Acceptance criteria

- The founder, social execution owner, paired seller, approver, Arabic reviewer, QA owner, and measurement owner are named or explicitly marked `[NEEDS INPUT]`.
- The active queue is capacity-sized and every item has an account/research source, buyer role, stage intent, language, evidence status, response owner, and review date.
- The active account set comes from `01-icp-abm`; every commercial interaction has a suppression check and does not override an active sales or legal restriction.
- Public copy preserves the founder's voice, contains no unsupported customer, performance, local-access, regulatory, capability, or competitor claim, and has the required approval record.
- Arabic/bilingual content has qualified native review; language results include a denominator, maturity state, unknown bucket, and pre-registered decision rule.
- Comments, DMs, requests, opt-outs, complaints, and commercial signals have documented routing, SLA, disposition, and escalation behavior.
- Social activity is linked to immutable account IDs and the canonical source taxonomy; organic diagnostics are reported separately from qualified pipeline and revenue.
- Sourced, influenced, and management-allocation views are not blended; immature cohorts are labeled provisional and unknown does not become zero.
- The workstream produces a weekly evidence pack and a dated scale, iterate, hold, or stop decision at the applicable GTM gate.
- Downstream recipients accept their handoffs, or the missing acceptance and blocker are recorded with an owner and date.
