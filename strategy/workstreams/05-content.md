# Workstream 05: Commercial Content

> **Status:** 14-day execution brief for an evidence-led content MVP. Not an approved editorial calendar or public claims library.
>
> **Evidence boundary:** The master GTM, Workstreams 01-03, and the available repository materials contain hypotheses, not validated customer evidence, performance benchmarks, pricing, named proof, or approved compliance language. Content must preserve those boundaries. Do not create assets to fill a quota or imply that publication proves demand.

## Objective

Create the smallest useful content system that helps the first Saudi B2B acquisition motion move a named account or qualified buyer from a recognized commercial problem to a credible next step. Content exists to support one offer, one buyer segment, and one measurable acquisition motion; it is not a separate traffic program.

The first decision this workstream supports is whether an evidence-backed content asset improves account progression, buyer comprehension, qualified conversations, or sales follow-up enough to continue, revise, or stop the motion. The first 14 days should produce reusable commercial assets and learning, not a high-volume publishing schedule.

Content must show the operating problem and the next decision across `FIND -> UNDERSTAND -> TARGET -> ENGAGE -> QUALIFY -> NURTURE -> CONVERT`. It must make the bounded Pipeline Reset and Acquisition Sprint understandable without promising guaranteed leads, revenue, speed, local access, or AI performance.

## Operating Principles

1. **Reader and decision first:** Every asset names the buyer role, account situation, question, and next commercial decision. A keyword alone is not a brief.
2. **One motion:** Choose the first asset set from the active account trigger and available access. Do not launch a broad channel mix because it is listed in the GTM.
3. **Evidence tags:** Mark claims as `Observed`, `Reported`, `Inferred`, `Hypothesis`, `Unknown`, or `Approved proof`. Keep source and date in the working brief.
4. **Commercial usefulness:** Prefer diagnostics, workflow teardowns, comparison guidance, pilot briefs, and sales follow-up assets over generic educational posts.
5. **No quota publishing:** Each candidate receives `Publish`, `Hold`, or `Cut` before production. Hold means a real buyer question exists but evidence or capability is missing; Cut means the idea is search-first filler without a useful reader decision.
6. **Human accountability:** AI may assist research, outlining, drafting, variant production, or synthesis only within the approved workflow. A human reviews every customer-facing asset.
7. **Language is a test:** Arabic-first, English-first, and bilingual versions are hypotheses to test by role, sector, trigger, and public language, not a universal localization rule.
8. **Measurement follows the handoff:** A download, click, or reply is not qualified pipeline. Record account progression, meeting held, accepted handoff, opportunity stage, and next milestone where instrumentation exists.

## Arabic / English Test

Run a controlled test on one buyer problem and one CTA. Keep the offer, proof boundary, audience rule, distribution window, and follow-up path constant. Vary only language treatment where possible:

- **Arabic-first:** human-reviewed Saudi Arabic version for accounts or roles where Arabic is the working/public language.
- **English-first:** English version for accounts and roles where English is the observed working/public language.
- **Bilingual:** paired or sequential treatment only where the account context supports it; do not assume that more languages improves conversion.

Record account ID, role, sector, trigger, language evidence, asset version, reviewer, send/publication date, source, permission, exposure, response, meeting held, accepted handoff, opportunity stage, and reason for no progression. Do not use opens, impressions, or raw clicks as the winning criterion. Do not declare a language winner from an underpowered or mixed-audience sample.

## Distribution

Distribution is account-led and permission-aware. Select only channels executable for the active tier and trigger.

| Route | Use | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Named-account outreach | Send a relevant excerpt or diagnostic to a sourced role after suppression and trigger checks. | ABM owner approves account, reason, role, source, date, and next milestone; seller owns response. |
| Founder or operator LinkedIn | Share one practical observation tied to the current motion and route relevant replies to the account owner. | No generic engagement bait, unsupported local claim, or unreviewed Arabic copy. |
| Permission-based email/nurture | Follow up on a requested asset, known relationship, event, partner, or consented interaction. | Marketing Ops enforces permission, suppression, frequency, source, and stage rules. |
| Partner or event distribution | Use a relevant partner, roundtable, office hour, or workflow clinic where the audience and handoff are defined. | Partner/event owner confirms audience, permission, recording/proof rules, and follow-up SLA. |
| Sales enablement | Put the diagnostic, FAQ, comparison guide, and sprint brief into discovery, proposal, and objection handling. | Sales confirms the asset answers a live objection and returns outcome data. |
| Search or website | Publish only when a named buyer question and conversion path justify discoverability. | SEO is secondary to usefulness; no thin keyword pages or orphaned assets. |

Do not use paid amplification, retargeting, mass outbound, or a new channel without an approved audience, consent/suppression posture, owner, budget, and measurable handoff. Workstream 01 owns account selection and suppression; this workstream supplies the asset and distribution brief.

## Content QA

Every asset passes the following gates before external use:

1. **Brief gate:** Named reader, trigger/problem, funnel stage, desired action, owner, distribution route, and decision it informs are recorded.
2. **Evidence gate:** Every material claim has a source, date, evidence label, confidence, and public-use status. Unknowns remain visible.
3. **Capability gate:** Scope, AI role, human review, pricing, timing, performance, local access, and outcome language match supplied capability and approved positioning. Unverified claims are removed or marked `[NEEDS INPUT]`.
4. **Competitive gate:** Comparisons use the Workstream 03 dimensions and buyer context. Do not reproduce competitor creative or imply effectiveness from visibility.
5. **Language gate:** A qualified human reviews Saudi Arabic customer-facing copy. Review meaning, register, terminology, cultural assumptions, and CTA clarity; do not rely on machine translation alone.
6. **Commercial gate:** CTA, qualification path, sales response SLA, client responsibility, and next milestone are executable and instrumented.
7. **Legal/privacy gate:** Consent, data use, customer proof, outreach channel, claims, confidentiality, and local/cross-border requirements are cleared or blocked by Legal.
8. **Usability gate:** Copy is clear, scannable, accessible, technically functional, and renders correctly in both directions where applicable. Links, forms, tracking, and owner routing are tested.
9. **Learning gate:** Version, audience, exposure window, hypotheses, and stop/continue rule are logged before distribution.

QA status must be one of `Draft`, `Blocked`, `Approved for internal use`, `Approved for external use`, or `Expired`. Approval has an owner and review date.

## Publish / Hold / Cut Gate

| Disposition | Use when | Required record |
|---|---|---|
| **Publish** | A named reader has a real question, the asset has enough evidence and capability support, and a measurable next step is executable. | Reader, source, claim status, reviewer, owner, route, CTA, and review date. |
| **Hold** | The reader/question is real but a missing proof, offer decision, language review, legal decision, or measurement field prevents responsible use. | Blocker, evidence needed, owner, trigger to unblock, and next review date. |
| **Cut** | The idea exists mainly to target a keyword, inflate volume, repeat an existing asset, or make an unsupported claim. | Reason for removal; do not queue it as a future quota item. |

## Outputs

1. **14-day MVP asset pack:** approved or clearly labeled drafts of the selected diagnostic, workflow teardown, sprint brief, comparison/FAQ asset, operator insight, and language test pair.
2. **Content brief and decision register:** reader, trigger, funnel stage, primary question, CTA, owner, evidence sources, disposition, and review date for every candidate.
3. **Evidence and claims register:** claim, source/date, label, confidence, permission, approved scope, expiry, and forbidden use.
4. **Distribution and routing plan:** account tier, role, channel, permission/suppression check, owner, response SLA, next milestone, and measurement field.
5. **Arabic/English test log:** variants, reviewer, routing basis, window, sample/decision rule, and qualified outcome fields.
6. **QA and revision log:** checklist result, blocker, approver, version, changes, and expiry/review date.
7. **Learning memo:** what buyers understood, which objections appeared, which asset or language version earned a qualified next step, contradictions, and the continue/revise/hold/cut decision.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each produced or proposed asset names a buyer role, account situation or trigger, question, funnel stage, CTA, owner, and measurable next action.
- The MVP is capacity-sized and contains no generic high-volume editorial quota; every candidate is marked `Publish`, `Hold`, or `Cut` with a reason.
- No public asset contains fabricated customer proof, pricing, performance, market statistics, local access, regulatory authority, customer quote, or capability claim.
- Material claims are traceable to a source and date, labeled by evidence status, and approved for the stated use or visibly marked as a hypothesis.
- The sprint brief, diagnostic, qualification language, and CTAs match the actual offer and the GTM handoff contract, or carry `[NEEDS INPUT]` blockers.
- Distribution is tied to an executable account, role, trigger, permission/suppression check, owner, response SLA, and route to the system of record.
- Arabic/English variants use the same commercial test frame, receive qualified human Arabic review, and have a predeclared sample/window/decision rule; no winner is declared from raw engagement alone.
- Content QA covers evidence, capability, competitive comparison, language, commercial routing, legal/privacy, usability, and learning metadata.
- Sales, ABM, Positioning, Competitive Intelligence, Marketing Ops/Analytics, Legal/QA, and the commercial owner have accepted the relevant handoff or recorded a blocker.
- The learning memo distinguishes content engagement from qualified account progression, accepted handoff, opportunity stage, and pipeline attribution.
- The workstream records unresolved inputs, owners, unblock triggers, and review dates before the Day 14 decision.
